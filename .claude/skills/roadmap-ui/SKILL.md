The idea is have a roadmap structure based on the actual implementation:
 - With edges to show prerequisites of Courses.
 - Click in the course shows prerequisites.
 - Dont use emojis
 - Mantain a soft dev-brutalism style for the page. Take the page https://italosalgado14.github.io/cv-automat/index.html as reference of what is a dev-brutalism (simple, with console colours).
