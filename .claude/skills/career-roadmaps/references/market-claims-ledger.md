# Market claims ledger

Derived from `market-sources.md`: one row per citable claim, so a roadmap's *Where the demand evidence points* block can cite exact figures with their date, horizon and geography. The FPGA rows (L12, L25, L38, L39) are ready for when that path gets its long-form document. Re-check a row against its source before reusing it after the next edition of that source.

Compiled 2026-09-26 for the roadmap revision. Every figure below was read on the page or in the PDF named in the URL column, on 2026-09-26, unless the row says otherwise.

How to use:
- Cite as "Publisher, Title (edition/date)". Keep the horizon and geography with the claim (market-sources.md rules 2 to 4).
- Status tags in the claim column: [PRIMARY] read in the publisher's own page or PDF; [PRIMARY, archived] publisher's page read through a Wayback copy because the live page blocked automated access; [SECONDARY] only a third-party summary was available; [NOT CROSS-CHECKED] value taken from a fetched summary of a data table and not confirmed on a second primary page.
- McKinsey Technology Trends Outlook 2026 (TTO) rows: job-posting data "drawn primarily from English-speaking countries" (TTO methodology). Do not present TTO posting changes as Chilean or German demand.
- Salary figures: this repo publishes none (README, catalog layer). The PwC wage-premium row is kept only as a record of what the source says.
- Contradicting signals are kept side by side on purpose (energy, quantum, semiconductors). Cite both sides or neither.
